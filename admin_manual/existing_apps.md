# Manage exiting Apps

## Access App details page:
1. Go to `User Apps` > `All Apps`
1. Search for App and click on it
1. Go the Versions section and click on `Details`of the chosen version

## Rebuild
Click `RESUBMIT APP VERSION`on the top right corner

## Find logs of a built App
Loggs of last build are under `BUILD` > `SHOW LOGS`

## Find docker of a build App
Find at the bottom of the page the tab `Dockerfile`

## Modify categories
Find `App categories` section and add and/or delete as see fit
